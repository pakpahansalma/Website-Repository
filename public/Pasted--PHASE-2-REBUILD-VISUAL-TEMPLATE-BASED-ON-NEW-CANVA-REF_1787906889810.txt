# 14. IMPORTANT ANALYTICAL POSITIONING

The portfolio should NOT overclaim business impact.

As a Data Analyst, the website should emphasize:

DATA → ANALYSIS → INSIGHT → DECISION SUPPORT

Do not automatically turn every insight into:

“increase revenue”

“improve retention”

“increase profit”

unless the evidence actually supports that conclusion.

For example:

Instead of:

“47% of customers were active for only one year, so the company should improve customer loyalty.”

Use:

“47% of customers were active for only one year, indicating a pattern of relatively short customer activity. This suggests an area for further investigation into repeat-purchase behavior, customer characteristics, and demand patterns.”

The website should distinguish between:

OBSERVATION
What the data shows.

INSIGHT
What the pattern may indicate.

NEXT QUESTION
What should be investigated further.

Do NOT present assumptions as facts.
